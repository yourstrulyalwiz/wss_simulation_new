---
name: Borrowing scope and assumptions
description: Point 5 requirements for forward-looking, entity-specific incremental borrowing.
---

Keep each entered area and sector as a separate borrowing entity. Equal entity names are labels, not permission to pool cash; derived national totals must not duplicate source loans.

**Why:** The user requires explicit pools, eligible streams, and no automatic cross-sector/cross-geography pooling.

**How to apply:** Aggregate numeric finance flows, retain source debt schedules as separate records, and export actual source loans once rather than again for the derived national view.

Size new loans only against established intervention net cash after relevant prior obligations, a coverage cushion, a user ceiling, and remaining investment need. Do not create recursive borrowing against hypothetical revenue from loan-financed connections. Keep all negative effects visible and implementation capex exclusively in the investment account.

Fixed contracted principal is an explicit forward-looking contract assumption, not delivery monitoring. It permits cash-forecast stress comparisons without automatically resizing agreed obligations. Without a fixed contract, the amount is a proposed loan, not an already-drawn facility.

**Why:** The user requires loan proceeds and cash allocations to change coverage, while contractual repayments remain fixed when cash falls.

**How to apply:** Recalculate physical purchases with loan drawdowns and only cash actually allocated to investment. Separate the reserve account, use reserves for shortfalls, release surplus only at maturity after obligations, and report unresolved debt/shortfalls without automatic refinancing.

Use nominal fixed payments with cash/principal converted using local inflation, then deflate reporting amounts. Existing saved rates retain their old real-rate meaning unless the user explicitly selects nominal. Beyond the projection horizon, this first release holds established cash and entered prior debt obligations flat in real terms and local inflation at the final projected rate.

**Why:** The user requires consistent real/nominal treatment, editable maturity, full schedules beyond the coverage horizon, and disclosure of conditional incremental—not full creditworthiness—estimates.

**How to apply:** Keep debt conversion independent of whether GDP is entered in real or nominal currency. Display projection-end outstanding debt and full maturity schedules with the assumptions.

When appending pages to a pruned/reordered PowerPoint template, do not assume the slide count provides a unique package-part address.

**Why:** The PowerPoint library's count-based naming can collide with surviving higher-numbered template parts, producing duplicate ZIP entries and potentially broken decks.

**How to apply:** Allocate unused slide-part addresses from the actual package, and verify exported ZIP entries are unique after adding appendices.
