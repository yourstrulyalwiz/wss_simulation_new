---
name: Connection revenue product boundaries
description: User-authorized baseline revenue feedback and empirical-cost boundaries.
---

Connection-based revenue is an optional **baseline model**, not an intervention.
It must apply to BAU as well as scenarios without merging tariff and collection
contributions or adding a duplicate dynamic-revenue contribution.

**Why:** The user explicitly rejected treating new-connection revenue as an
intervention. Billing depends on delivered customers, not SM coverage alone.

**How to apply:** Keep baseline configuration distinct from intervention toggles;
preserve existing capital/funded-asset/service hierarchy corrections. Do not
equate cash credited to financing with the amount of financing gap closed.

Empirical billing shares, household-volume share and operating-cost assumptions
must come from observed data or documented assumptions, never invented defaults.
The user now authorizes equivalent annual per-billed-household costs, manual
annual household costs and a deliberately accepted average-expenditure proxy.
Only one basis is authoritative. The proxy needs compatible raw real-currency
annual expenditure, explicit household allocation and a frozen baseline customer
denominator; a volume share is not a known cost share.

**Why:** The 2026-10-06 simplified-connection instruction explicitly authorized
these options while rejecting invented cost data and a complete utility-account
interpretation. Unbilled customers can still incur operating costs.

**How to apply:** Require explicit funding-reference assumptions and provenance;
do not infer costs or baseline utility revenue from GDP, funding or replacement.
Use the consolidated NRW reconciliation policy for tagged overlap; do not
restore a separate gross NRW credit or invent a recovered-water production cost.

Keep baseline calibration separate from future billed participation. SM upgrades
replace the billing status of their eligible opening Basic cohort, rather than
adding every upgraded household as a new customer. Newly delivered Basic
households cannot upgrade in the same year; closing billed stock earns recurring
cash and incurs recurring cost beginning next year.
**Why:** The user requires lower future participation to reduce revenue rather
than being cancelled by higher inferred consumption, and mixed Basic cohorts
need their actual weighted prior billing mix rather than a constant assumption.
**How to apply:** Preserve frozen calibration/reference assumptions across
independent BAU, scenario and no-loan calculations; keep affordability and billing
cohorts separate. Allow signed billing/cost-reference reductions. Keep physical
NRW-origin households distinct from incremental tagged billing equivalents.
