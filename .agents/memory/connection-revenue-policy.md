---
name: Aggregate connection revenue policy
description: October 8 replacement of household calibration and operating costs.
---
Use aggregate marginal delivered-coverage expansion over fixed baseline service
shares, scaled by the existing total billed-volume path. Pure BAU disables this
feature; an enabled connection-only scenario runs. Combine signed Basic-to-SM
differences before flooring the entire candidate at zero. Valid overlap can remove
only the positive increment, never baseline volume. Use the prior observation
year's household denominator and one-year lag.

**Why:** The 2026-10-08 user specification explicitly supersedes the prior
household calibration, funding-reference declarations and all connection-cost
methods. The funding/repayment follow-on later that day supersedes negative
connection adjustments with addition-only revenue. Preserve the aggregate
customer mix, including non-household sales.

**How to apply:** Do not revive historical billing shares, consumption calibration,
cost proxies, household revenue haircuts or activation confirmations. Preserve
old values only as inactive audit metadata. Direct connection and NRW sales use
baseline rates; collection then tariff apply to all reconciled volume. Connections
are now selectable for loans, but existing selections must not acquire them.
