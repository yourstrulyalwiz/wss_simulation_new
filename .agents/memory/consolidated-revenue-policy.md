---
name: Consolidated revenue product boundaries
description: Approved cost-basis, delivery and eligibility choices from the consolidated 2026-10-06 instruction.
---

NRW implementation costs remain inside signed NRW net cash and inside the
selected loan-source pool. The proposal to relocate costs to a separate
requirements ledger or size loans on recurring-only NRW cash was withdrawn.
Scheduled physical recovery must not acquire a cash-availability gate.
**Why:** The revised user instruction explicitly rejected both cost-treatment
changes; they can otherwise silently change borrowing eligibility and duplicate
financing shortfalls.
**How to apply:** Preserve after-cost source cash and floor only the combined
selected signed pool. Do not interpret service-impact chart bands as source cash.

Targets are requirement benchmarks, not delivery ceilings. Opening Basic
households are shared by NRW, ordinary funded SM and affordability delivery,
in that order; new Basic entrants cannot transition again in the same year.
**Why:** The approved example requires 250k NRW upgrades from 600k SM to 850k SM
despite an 800k target.
**How to apply:** Keep minimum-service requirement targets and pricing unchanged,
while limiting actual delivery by eligible pools rather than target room.

The approved default is one affordability offer per newly eligible cohort.
Unknown NRW/public-funded household identities are deducted proportionally by
income band; known affordability outcomes stay in their actual band.
**Why:** Reapplying take-up every year to remaining households or deriving
entrants from net Basic stock would duplicate offers or miss new entrants.
**How to apply:** Re-offering is a separately specified future policy, not a
silent recalibration of the default. Self-financers remain excluded
counterfactuals rather than credited SM delivery.

Recovered-water sales and sewer eligibility remain scenario assumptions, not
observed demand or customer identities. Legacy full-sales and full returned-water
eligibility assumptions must be visible and editable.
**Why:** Physical recovery alone does not establish billable demand or
sanitation customer overlap; the user required transparent assumptions.
**How to apply:** Tag water overlap by the incremental SM-versus-Basic billed
share, never all new connections. Require identified sewer overlap rather than
guessing it from unrelated sanitation connection growth.
