---
name: Exogenous volume release boundary
description: Preserve exogenous growth and defer customer/consumption-driven revenue feedback.
---

Keep connection-revenue feedback outside this release. Preserve existing entered billed
volume and NRW system-volume growth assumptions: population ratio once OR a fixed rate,
never both. Zero fixed growth means flat volume. Funded coverage must not implicitly
change the volume forecast, recurring operating costs, or borrowing cash.

Basic-to-safely-managed upgrades are not automatically new paying customers. Any future
extension must distinguish customer/billing transitions from physical service transitions
and explicitly model consumption and operating costs.

**Why:** The user explicitly deferred endogenous feedback and requires no new-customer
assumption for upgrades and no double application of population growth.

**How to apply:** Maintain a separate exogenous volume-projection boundary feeding the
revenue ledger. Document the anchors, mutually exclusive growth options, and deferred
extension without implementing it or adding recursive loan-funded revenue.
