---
name: Optional forecast assumptions pause
description: Scope and preservation rules for temporarily disabling custom scalar forecast assumptions.
---

Keep optional forecast assumptions temporarily deactivated.

**Why:** The user asked to deactivate this feature for now, not remove saved
simulation data or disable automatic forecasting.

**How to apply:** Pause the custom GDP-fallback and ongoing inflation overrides
in both controls and calculations, retaining their saved values for later
re-enabling. Continue automatic projections with the established defaults and
honor entered year-by-year data. Do not broaden the pause to service targets,
budget forecasts, or intervention assumptions. Re-enable the controls and
calculation behavior together only when requested.
