---
name: Funded assets and outstanding expansion
description: User-approved gross funded asset proxy and carry-forward accounting semantics.
---

Unfunded expansion is outstanding household transitions, never assets. Funded
expansion is delivered within the year and generates replacement starting next
year. Use gross funded capital as the asset proxy; replacement maintains assets,
and unpaid replacement does not imply deterioration or service failure.

**Why:** The user supplied the expanded accounting correction to prevent
outstanding work from generating fictitious maintenance and repeated funding need.

**How to apply:** Keep annual planned work, closing outstanding work, and annual
unpaid replacement/negative cash distinct. Endline requirement is closing expansion
plus accumulated annual shortfalls, not a sum of yearly outstanding balances.
Preserve independent BAU/scenario calculations and physical NRW reuse without
crediting its service value as sector cash.

## Meaning of paid work in reporting

“Paid” means actual current-year cash funding, not merely the capital credited
against a target or the value of physical infrastructure reuse.

**Why:** The user requested paid replacement and expansion amounts alongside
pre-funding requirements to understand why unfinished work carries into the next
year. Confusing cash spending with target credit can give a misleading explanation.

**How to apply:** Keep replacement payments separate from expansion payments:
replacement maintains existing assets and does not clear unfinished expansion.
Expansion cash can include household finance and advance delivery; physical reuse,
target changes and repricing can also change the closing expansion balance, so do
not promise that cash paid alone explains every opening-to-closing difference.

## Legacy obligations must remain qualified

Accumulating an unpaid-replacement diagnostic does not itself establish a
legitimate, settleable deferred obligation. Until a separate correction verifies
carry-forward and repayment behavior, financial reporting must identify that
component as a legacy measure and qualify totals containing it. Ancillary
allowances similarly need their actual calibration/status explained.

**Why:** The user's ledger instructions explicitly distinguish explaining the
current balance from validating replacement carryover or ancillary assumptions.

**How to apply:** Add exact reporting breakdowns where needed, but do not invent
missing allocations, repayments or corrected totals through frontend arithmetic.
Show shared/unallocated obligations separately when attribution is unavailable.
