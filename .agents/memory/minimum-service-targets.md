---
name: Minimum service target semantics
description: User-approved hierarchy for safely managed and basic access objectives.
---

Treat service targets as minimum achievements: safely managed service satisfies
the basic minimum, but basic service cannot satisfy a safely managed shortfall.
Keep coverage categories mutually exclusive and preserve the original target
pathway for reporting. Assess deficits within each modeled geography and year;
surplus in another geography must not erase a local deficit.

**Why:** The user's 70 SM / 30 basic target and 80 SM / 20 basic example must not
require restoring the basic-only category to 30. That would demand 110 served
households out of 100. Their supplied instruction explicitly separates this
reporting issue from legitimate replacement and other financial obligations.

**How to apply:** Distinguish exclusive basic-only diagnostics from combined
at-least-basic entry requirements. In the staged pathway, 60 SM / 30 basic against
70/30 still requires ten basic entries and ten SM upgrades. Do not subtract SM
surplus twice from a combined objective or change delivery ceilings merely to
implement this hierarchy.

Sector-wide financing displays must make their combined SM/basic scope explicit,
even when placed beside SM-only coverage graphs.
**Why:** The user interpreted achievement of the SM target as closing the entire
sector financing need; basic-entry obligations can still remain.
**How to apply:** Preserve the distinction between combined financing and
single-service coverage or attributed financing when labeling graphs.
