name: Post-intervention analysis
description: User constraints for target revisions and additional funding after interventions.
---
Post-intervention options are a separate analysis, not a replacement for the saved original targets or intervention design. Untouched and reset targets must retain the full original schedule, including intermediate targets.

**Why:** The user requested comparison of revised ambitions and remaining financing without changing the original simulation.

**How to apply:** Preserve these boundaries in saving, restoring comparisons and future reporting. Loan sizing remains anchored to the original-target reference scenario; revised targets and trial funding must not resize it.

Additional funding is an execution-adjusted financing equivalent, independent of public budget execution and capital share. A coverage-achieved result alone is not a fully financed result.

**Why:** The supplied instructions require real-engine replay of both coverage thresholds and all remaining financial obligations. Historical cash shortfalls retain their existing no-repayment treatment.

**How to apply:** Never label a residual/GDP division or unverified candidate as the solution. Retain explicit delivery, convergence, scope and financing limitations rather than silently treating unavailable results as zero.
