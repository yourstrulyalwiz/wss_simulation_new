---
name: Programme financing timing
description: User-defined separation of investment shortfalls, cash carry, and service gaps.
---

Compare each year's independently scheduled expansion/upgrades, replacement, and distinct implementation capex with eligible financing once. Do not spend financing on a coverage backlog and then subtract it again from the cost of the remaining backlog. Service gaps are coverage snapshots, not investment requirements.

Carry unused investment financing forward without interest, starting at zero. Count opening carry as availability, never as a new receipt. Later surpluses do not retroactively cancel prior annual shortfalls, and missed costs are not automatically rescheduled.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires conservation of carried cash and separate annual shortfalls, cumulative programme requirements, and terminal unmet coverage. Repeated backlog or cash-balance snapshots must not become additional cost or financing flows.

**How to apply:** Reconcile opening cash plus new financing (plus separately reported unfunded signed outflows) to financing applied plus closing cash. Sum scheduled annual costs and shortfalls; show period-end closing cash rather than summed cash balances. Keep debt-service cash reserves separate until explicitly released. Distinguish enabling intervention implementation capex from household upgrade costs already scheduled in the target programme.
