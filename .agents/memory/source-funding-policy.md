---
name: Source funding attribution
description: Accounting precedence and unchanged-delivery boundary for actual funded coverage.
---

Absorb signed cash losses proportionally from positive ordinary sources, pay
contractual debt from originally selected sources only, then pay replacement
from the whole remaining ordinary pool before allocating that payment to sources.
Attribute actual combined purchases proportionally, using ordinary cash before
restricted loan proceeds. Retain signed revenue without negative purchase weights.

**Why:** The consolidated October 8 instructions reject source-specific gross
replacement obligations and replay attribution: those can manufacture shortfalls
or change delivery solely because of accounting order. Only selected-source debt
payment restrictions are authorized to change aggregate financing/delivery.

**How to apply:** Preserve combined revenue equations and forward lag. Keep losses,
unfunded debt and unfunded replacement separate; never deduct the same signed loss
twice. Loans fund expansion only. Cost savings stay embedded in purchases, while
physical upgrades and external household financing have explicit origins.
