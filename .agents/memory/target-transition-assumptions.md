---
name: Annual target transition assumptions
description: Rationale for gross household flows and the simplified target asset schedule.
---

Aggregate service milestones do not uniquely identify individual household movements. The target programme assumes net new households adopt the current year's target mix and calculates continuing-household transitions separately. Do not revert to identifying upgrades solely from falling Basic counts: population growth can conceal those upgrades.

**Why:** Net-count transitions can omit new household connections when total serviced counts fall, and can misattribute existing Basic assets after upgrades. Gross connections and incremental upgrades must be priced once, with prior Basic asset value transferred rather than purchased again.

**How to apply:** Explain this population-cohort assumption in the Guide. Preserve count-CAGR interpolation where defined, use linear growth for zero-start categories, and honor exact milestone distributions. Replacement remains a simplified allowance on retained booked assets, without automatic asset retirement or spare-capacity reuse; do not silently introduce those assumptions or confuse this schedule with simulated coverage assets.
