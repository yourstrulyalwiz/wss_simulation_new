World Bank Water Data — Design System & Theme Prompt

Apply the following design system consistently across all pages and components in this project. This is the established visual language for the World Bank Water Data platform.

Typography
Primary font: Noto Sans (Google Fonts). Import via @import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Noto+Sans:ital,wght@0,400;0,500;0,600;0,700;0,800;1,400&display=swap');
Set font-family: 'Noto Sans', ui-sans-serif, system-ui, sans-serif on the root body.
Heading treatment: All major page and section headings use a split bold/light pattern. The first word or phrase is font-extrabold in navy #014972; the second word or phrase is font-normal in muted gray #9aa6ac. Example: <span class="font-extrabold">FEATURED</span> <span class="font-normal text-[#9aa6ac]">APPLICATIONS</span>.
Headings are always uppercase with tracking-tight.
Hero headings: text-4xl md:text-5xl.
Section headings: text-3xl md:text-4xl.
Card titles: text-lg to text-2xl font-extrabold text-[#014972].
Body/description text: text-lg leading-relaxed text-[#4C809C] for hero descriptions; text-sm leading-6 text-[#617078] for card and supporting text.
Labels, eyebrows, filter group titles: text-xs font-extrabold uppercase tracking-wider text-[#014972].

Tone & Copy Style
No emojis anywhere.
All navigation labels and major headings in uppercase.
Descriptions are one to two professional sentences, written in third-person institutional voice.
No lorem ipsum — all placeholder text should be realistic water sector / World Bank content.
Numbers and targets are always highlighted in teal text-[#009CA7] font-extrabold when they appear inline in body text.
