7. Remaining assumptions and limitations

  • Forward-looking scenarios only: no delivery monitoring, catch-up replanning or automatic rescheduling of missed investment.
  • Replacement is an opening-stock allowance, not asset-cohort retirement modelling; retained assets and downgrade/upgrade book transfers are approximate.
  • Volumes remain exogenous. Use population growth once or a fixed rate, not both. Funded connections/upgrades do not automatically become paying customers; endogenous consumption, operating costs and connection-revenue feedback are deferred.
  • The borrowing module is an incremental financing estimate, not a full utility credit assessment. Entered streams, prior obligations and fixed contracts determine its cash capacity and conditional disclosures.
  • Committed reserves earn no interest. Unpaid contractual service/debt remains visible; no automatic refinancing or cross-pool subsidy is assumed.
  • Debt is tracked through maturity beyond the coverage horizon. Post-horizon established cash and prior obligations are flat in real terms, with final projected inflation held constant; coverage is not extended to match debt maturity.
  • National totals sum local flows and unmet gaps; surplus coverage or cash in one area cannot silently meet another area's obligations.
  • Older unsupported saves default to reinvest-all/no-new-borrowing and retain previous choices for review. Missing rate basis retains legacy real-rate meaning with a warning rather than guessing nominal intent.
  • Sanitation remains a simplified service/capital structure, not a separate full on-site/FSM/sewered treatment-sizing model.
