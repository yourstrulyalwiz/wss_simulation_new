Indicative loan funding

Implementation and verification · 6 October 2026

Indicative loan proceeds — repayment accounting deferred. Loan sizing uses the selected year's additional net cash and assumes equal annual repayments. Principal and interest payments are not deducted from model funding in this version.

Implemented behavior

  • One forecast year supplies the frozen intervention-only cash reference and receives one loan injection.
  • Signed selected collection, tariff and NRW cash is pooled before applying the zero floor, allocation and annuity factor. Existing eligible sanitation-linked NRW cash is counted once; standalone connection cash is excluded.
  • Principal, interest, fees and affordability verification are deferred. Annual allocation is a sizing assumption, not a funding deduction.
  • Restricted cash follows existing investment allocation and delivery constraints. Opening unspent cash + new injection = loan-funded investment + closing unspent cash.
  • Separate sector/area loans retain independent timing and terms. National roll-ups add actual cash flows without averaging loan assumptions.
  • Legacy maturity spans supply valid migrated terms; old grace, repayment structure and ceiling remain inactive metadata. New blank rates and terms are not invented defaults.
  • Results defaults loan-excluded; explicit inclusion updates calculations without deleting saved settings. Scenario funding reads its own cash arrays.
  • Calculator, Results, CSV, Excel, both PowerPoint paths and proceeds-table downloads identify deferred repayment accounting.

Scope and limitations

This is a gross financing scenario, not lender underwriting or a net benefit after financing costs. Principal, interest, fees and affordability remain intentionally unmodeled; zero execution deductions mean deferred accounting, not free finance or a repaid loan. Existing replacement-accounting, ancillary-calibration and full-versus-incremental SM upgrade-price qualifications remain unchanged. Existing build chunk-size and Pydantic namespace advisories remain non-blocking.
